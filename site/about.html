The Samudio way

Old-school values. Modern service.

Jayden believes auto repair should be simple: honest pricing, clear communication, and work done right.

Samudio Auto is fully mobile. Instead of arranging a ride and sitting in a waiting room, you call or text — and Jayden brings the tools to your driveway, your workplace or the side of the road.

His goal is to help you feel safe and confident on the road, without overcharging you or pushing repairs you don’t need.
